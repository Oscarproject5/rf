## FAQ Section

### 1. How much does water testing cost?
Our comprehensive 15-point water test is completely free with no obligation. We believe you should know what's in your water before making any decisions about treatment systems.

### 2. What's included in the warranty?
Tank warranties range from 10-25 years depending on the system. Electronic components carry 5-year warranties. Labor and service warranties are 1 year. See full warranty terms for complete details and exclusions.

### 3. How long does installation take?
Most residential installations complete in 2-4 hours. Whole-home systems may require 4-6 hours. We schedule convenient times that work with your family's schedule and minimize disruption to your daily routine.

### 4. Do you service other brands?
Yes, our technicians service and maintain most major water treatment brands. We can also upgrade existing systems with improved components. Contact us for specific model compatibility and service pricing.

### 5. What financing options are available?
We offer 0% financing for qualified customers with flexible payment terms up to 60 months. Monthly rental programs are also available for customers who prefer not to purchase. Credit approval and terms vary.

### 6. How often do filters need changing?
Filter replacement schedules vary by system type and water usage. Typical schedules are:
- Sediment filters: every 3-6 months
- Carbon filters: every 6-12 months  
- RO membranes: every 2-3 years
We provide automatic reminder service and can handle all maintenance.

### 7. Will this work with my existing plumbing?
Our systems are designed to integrate with standard residential plumbing. Our technicians perform a pre-installation survey to identify any requirements and ensure proper installation.

### 8. What happens if I move?
Our warranties are transferable to new owners, which can increase your home's value. We also provide system removal and reinstallation services if you want to take your system to your new home.
